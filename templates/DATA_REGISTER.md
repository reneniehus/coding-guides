# data/: inputs and their provenance

Only a source's own fetch, freeze or de-identification script writes here, into its own
folder; nothing here is edited by hand. Each delivery, live download or frozen extract is a
dated vintage folder, never overwritten, with a manifest.json (each file's sha256 and row
count, source commits or URLs, rules, corrections, source files that gave no rows) that the
loader verifies on every load. Field meanings are in
[data_overview.md](../documentation/data_overview.md).

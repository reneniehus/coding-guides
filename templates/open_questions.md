# Open questions

Decisions that wait for the owner. Each lists the facts it depends on, every number linked to
the committed table or page behind it, and carries no options, ranking or recommendation
unless the owner asks for them. A dated entry in [decisions.md](decisions.md) closes a
question, which is then deleted here (git keeps it).
